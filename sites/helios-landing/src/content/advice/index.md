title: 'Advice'
---

I am still very young, but here are some things that have helped me and that I think are worth sharing.

- Always be willing to learn things. I think the most successful people are always willing to learn about new things even if it isn't in their domain. I spent a few weeks learning how to edit videos for fun and can now use that skill forever. 
- If you start a company, don't spend the early days trying to make everything perfect or micromanaging every detail. You'll only make the initial workload feel much harder than it needs to be. I learnt this the hard way.
- (*Originally from my business teacher, but something I also learned through experience.*) Don't start a company with your friends for the sake of being friends with them. Only do it if they're genuinely the right fit.
- Strategy > Plans. Not every scenario is going to be what you planned for, it's only strategies that can help you deal with them. This is applicable to everything.
- Be good at saying stuff. Don't be a pathological liar but be able to say something on the spot. You'll thank me later after an interview.
- Talk to people. Best case scenario you get an opportunity. Worst case scenario you leave with a connection.
- Read stuff. I am not a book reader but I do love reading research papers and forums about questions I may have. Sure AI is great at things like this but I also do think reading is necessary to an extent.
- Watch YouTube documentaries. 
- Don't be afraid to fail because you probably will at some point but you still leave with impact and something you have learnt forever.
- Build shit even if it's just for the love of the game.

If I find more advice this will be the place. Also if you want to talk about this advice you know where to find me.
