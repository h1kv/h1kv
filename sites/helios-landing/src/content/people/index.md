This is a list of people I find inspiring or simply worth mentioning.

- [Garry Newman](https://garry.net/) made some of my favourite games. His blog is also an interesting read especially when you are bored. Probably the reason why I tried to enter the game industry.
- [Alan Becker](https://www.youtube.com/@alanbecker) is the only person I ever bought merch from. I used to love (*still do*) his animations as a kid. He still makes absolutely amazing videos to this day.
- [Aidan Ouckama](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ouck/) was openly honest about his internship journey and just has funny humour when it comes to tech. I love his brainrot videos about tech.
- [Sam Altman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Altman)'s startup advice still sticks to me to this day.
- [Carter Abdallah](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carter-abdallah/)'s personal advice and thoughts of the world have been something I personally agree with. He's thoughts on college are also very important to me. He also got acquired by NVIDIA which is inspirational in itself. 
- **Damien Allen** was my old computer science teacher. Made me realise that some teachers are just genuinely intrigued in the subjects they teach and when that's the case, they teach incredibly well. Also pushed me to be my best in the tech world.
- [Socially Inept](https://sociallyinept.io/) are such a funny trio. I hope to see them in the SF tour one day.
- [Patrick Collison](https://patrickcollison.com/about)'s website is what inspired this one. I also like his self-made journey. 
- [Sean Gahan](https://www.tiktok.com/@seanygahan) genuinely has some inspirational advice. His mindset is something that I inspire to have at some point. "Don't live for someone else" is a good watch.

*I feel this page will probably grow as I get older.*
