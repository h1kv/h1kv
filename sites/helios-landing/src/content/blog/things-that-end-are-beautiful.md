I mentioned previously in day 10 that we as normal humans think an average 60,000+ thoughts per day, but here comes the realisation.. You only know a handful of thoughts for that previous day, but why? I theorise that thoughts and memories are weighed subconsciously by the human mind, regardless of self-intervention, the weight determines how important the thoughts and memories may be, but there is one more thing. They determine what represents that day, for example a simple act of kindness may be part of the only handful of thoughts and memories that represent your previous day. Then here is where it gets interesting because what thoughts and memories would represent your week? your month? your year? All you can say for sure is only a handful because that is the truth, if we process 60,000 thoughts per day, imagine how much you process in a week? Now, let me say that my theory isn’t about what I’ve been talking rather I have introduced you to the foundations of what I’m about to say. What factor is single-handily a certain assurance that a memory or thought will be naturally weighed in favour to represent that specific timeframe more than others with self-intervention? Personally, I believe it’s the knowledge of knowing something isn’t going to last. Everything ends in this world, someway or somehow but its so normal that its became a engraved system in the human-mind, but if you were to be given the knowledge that something as simple as going to a event with strangers isn’t going to last, then instantly the thoughts and memories are naturally weighed in favour to represent the timeframe. Let me explain with an own personal example of mine to further strengthen this. I went on a bus-tour to a tourist site in my home country, I knew that this was going to end because it was a one day trip, I met some very interesting and nice strangers but still I knew it would end. We had our own thoughts and emotions about one another, regardless of positivity or negativity I still knew it was going to end. We shared laughs, previous experiences, but I still knew it was going to end. My mind may have thought so many things unrelated to these people, but yet they still somehow manage to be part of the handful of thoughts and memories that represented the day, and maybe even the week? Who knows, I am just saying what I personally have been thinking about. Thank you for listening. Goodnight, love helios <3
