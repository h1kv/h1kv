It is neaely the end of the summer, can you imagine that?


The last good amount of weeks before I did that post when I was in Dogpatch Labs a lot has changed my life, like quite litterally a lot. I decided to do a post today because you know I never really like got that feeling to do so until today. A lot of things have happened both in a negative and positive manner. So I will list out some of the events for my future self to either regret upon or just to laugh about. 


- **AI engineering intern @ Dubsof** : Probably one of the coolest things I've ever done in my life. The work there is really cool and the volatile enviromment of a startup really has put an impact on what I really want to do in the future of my life. Also the co-workers there make the experience so much more amazing. They want me to work next summer possibly go full time? We shall see though. 
- **Got an offer to a great school, and declined it** : I recently got the chance (oops this sounds like a generic LinkedIn starter post lol) to be given the offer to study at a private school that costs 13,000 euros just for my final year. It offers so much new subjects I wish my new school offered. Although as much as I was an original advocate for it; I do think things have changed situationally and the factors of things do make me realise stuff differently. I was told by a good friend and few others that I will do absolutely fine and even if not I am very talented. I also partly wanted to leave because I felt like I had no friends in my current school but the amount of (and unexpected) sadness from friends whom I didn't think would be upset really shaped things for me.
- **I applied to Y Combinator.** : Me and another friend have applied to YC. It is a very scary moment because if we do get in, pass the final interview imagine the trajectory of our lives. Things wil be changed so rapidly. Terms like SAFE, FMF and PMF are some abberivations that I had just learnt because of YC. I do plan to apply again if we don't make it this time. 
- **Lost the girl I knew for five years** : I look at it still as this has only happened recently that they do well in life. As corny as that stuff does sound - they genuinely do deserve something of a better life. I do hope sometimes that I shaped it for the better in someways. If you are that person reading this, you know I will always be here and I am sorry for my side of things. 
- **Bootstrapped a startup that I am actually proud of** : Me and some friends started an AI company in developing agentic testing by harnessing human-releastic systems. Unicorn status one day? Hope so. We've went through a lot of shit in this company from betrayals, declined offers, founder-exits but we also did some amazing engineering feat that 10 year old me would call fake. 
- **Patch and OpenAI** : Was a part of the builders week for 5 days. I presented something I was proud of building to a group of people from all places and sorts of things. I was genuinely so scared. I overcame my fears so I am happy. 
- **I spent some more time, like real-life time with my bestfriend** : We watched some cool movies such as the new spiderman one and also had Popeyes. W for choosing the store location in this shite hole of a town, Portlaoise. I am joking. Portlaoise was my intro into independance for myself, now it's Dublin. 
- **I am unsure about college for once** : I have always been hardcoded to go get a Bsc, then to Msc but I don't know now. There is so much factors that I am actually unsure about you know? I don't know man there's like so much choices for me to do I am actually unsure. 
- **Myself in Dublin.** : I'll admit it at 17 years old I had near no experience with being alone in a city like Dublin, had no clue how the Luas works, the Train, etc. Now I am going there like its nothing and even guess what? Cycling in a busy fucking city. I am proud of it and no matter how embarassing it may be I am glad. It has actually convinced me to live in Dublin, lmao. 


but yep. that is all i have to say and that was all within 2 months. there is alot to digest there even for myself and i do think i am missing stuff because i am writing this stuff in a terrible headspace at the moment. 


anyhow, this is my end here.
