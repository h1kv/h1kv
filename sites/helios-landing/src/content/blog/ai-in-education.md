**tldr; ai in education absolutely sucks if you dont care about your future, however for those who do ai is one of the best tools and implementation and learning how to use these tools in a effective manner is needed skill**

i have noticed alot of people use GPT now for homework, as a "get it done in time" sort of thing yknow? in this sense i could come home, prompt into GPT the homework, and be done in the next ten minutes. 

and.. that's why I entirely understand why there is a growing demand for the DOE and schools to take GPT usage into a "ban", with attempts to mitigate. 

### however ai is so useful, if USED RIGHT

if you use ai to help you learn, help you with problems, provide solutions and not just easy homework done type of stuff, then you are already using ai for the better, for example i used ai to read my teacher's business powerpoint, and ask me 20 questions and score me based upon my instruction set that i had given it (that co-aligns with marking schemes). this in a way makes you want to retain the information, learn, read, then test. sure studyclix, examrevision, do that but they do not do it at the level in which GPT can do. 

What do you mean I can receive personal feedback per question on the fly? What do you mean I can receive where I went wrong, what marks I lost out on, recommendations to my answers? like thats fucking crazy and no other platform from what i know does it.
