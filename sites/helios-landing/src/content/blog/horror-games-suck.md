the one thing i find really terrible about most modern day horror games now is that they are so predictable 

why is it that i know when i get jumpscared *nothing* is going to happen for the next five minutes? why does the horror game get less scary as time goes on because the jumpscares become mundane? and for the games that try to surge your adrenline with a chase scene (like **outlast trials**), why is it after a while it becomes default muscle memory and not something that scary? 

### roblox horror games suck even more
sure what i am complaining about is nowhere near the level of how **SHIT** roblox horror games can be, when i was a kid i fell in love with the genre of "SCP" and "Area-51" styled games because they offered replayability (which in most cases, including **AAA** games do not and follow a storyline) but also because i was a kid back then. 

looking at the games i use to play now and the current state of roblox horror games, its a copy paste type of thing and i feel like the quality of them have downsided so much to the point that genre is turning into what mobile games are (**cash grabs..**) 

### so what makes a horror game good then? 
first of all i need to say horror games that follow a storyline are shit. im not against story games, hell i love them. but when it comes to the horror genre its not really that fun. sure if you add replayability like **barotrauma** and **outlast trials** it becomes fun to some extent 

but i have came up with a list of what I think makes horror games great 

- unpredictable 
- has you tensed or kept on edge 24/7
- replayable but also story-adjcent 
- doesn't just spam jumpscares and hope you get scared 
- follows different style horror than triple A do 
- an actually good quality game 

for example i recently watched a 1 hour video of a game that has for certain 1000+ pages of lore and mechanics but i was fucking hooked [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sFJ5LrDIzes&t](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sFJ5LrDIzes&t)

### what makes voice of the void actually good though
it doesn't follow horror tradition and it is random everytime you play so in contrast that gives you already three things:
- replayable (and story adjcent) 
- unpredictable 
- doesn't follow triple A
