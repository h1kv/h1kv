You know after my fair share of making stuff I think it came to a point where I wanted to document those things. I dont really run any "blog" pages in the traditional sense and I dont really talk about these things to people. 

So.. I decided why not just write whatever comes to my mind here? I dont really plan on following this stuff religously, ill probably post shit randomly when the time comes to it. 

If you haven't realised, my blog UI and what not is heavily inspired by **Garry Newman**. Who played a massive role in my life with Gmod and Rust. ([you can see his blog here](https://garry.net)). 

### So what will I write here? 
I dont know. I am only a 16 year old so I do plan that things I say here may change overtime. I don't know when Garry did his posts and what age he was but damn 22 years ago was his first. I know that I probably will look back at this stuff and "cringe" over the shit I said as I matured, alongside my terrible grammar. 

### Vibe coding as a product manager 
20% of the codebase was vibecoded but 100% of the codebase was designed with redudancy. I know later on in few years or so ima look at the source code and wonder how the fuck I made this work. I tried to limit that just a little bit by making it all modular so I can just slot shit out as time goes. 

### Posts and religion 
You know some people take blogging a very serious thing and try to post every day or every week and you know it is what it is. As long as it works and their happy. I honestly just plan to post whenever my mind comes with something interesting I want to share for myself in the future to read. I dont really plan many people to see this unless they do.
