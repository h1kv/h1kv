I couldn't help but notice that starting something fresh again feels really good. Like really really good. Whatever it could be and whether it is restarting from scratch (*which mainly is the case*) the feeling feels so fresh and feels as if some sort of pressure (*although even if there isn't*)  


This situation has happened to me in friendships. Video games. Side projects. Video edits. Homework?. Organising my room. 

It quite literally appears to happen almost everywhere in some way for me. My guess for games adding a rebirthing system is to allow this sort of feeling to happen whilst rewarding the player. 

It was only a few weeks ago that this happened to me at work. Although the reason may not be that because we got to Lvl 100 and rebirthed to get a increased stat multiplier, it was mainly due to the fact that startups love to shift things and what happens next week is not certainly predictable. 

It quite literally felt like it was a video game rebirth though. The system we redesigned entirely outperformed the older system by 10x all whilst being less lines of code and more understanding if you had to ELI5 it to someone. Sadly due to "employee agreed confidentiality" I won't go into detail on what changed and how it improved but let's just say that the situation was just like a video game rebirth. 

I also wondered is there a science for this happening? For when we restart something from scratch or essentially "start from zero" why do we feel a positive emotion only? Or am I just some idiot who only feels this way. 

and, I was correct. There is plenty of research papers that hypothesize and try to explain the reasoning for this to happen. My personal favourite one though is: *Consider It Done! Plan Making Can Eliminate the Cognitive Effects of Unfulfilled Goals"* You cand the exact NLM here: *Masicampo EJ, Baumeister RF. Consider it done! Plan making can eliminate the cognitive effects of unfulfilled goals. J Pers Soc Psychol. 2011 Oct;101(4):667-83. doi: 10.1037/a0024192. PMID: 21688924.*

It's the belief that a tangled project, which by the way is usually the case in large peices of software (*particularly in this AI era where GitHub is seeing a increase in AI commits*) usually includes a lot of disconnected TODOs, things you're unsure of and assumptions. However restarting from zero allows someone to create a standardised set of goals and drops all the previous mental noise from "old me". 

I quite enjoy that reason.
