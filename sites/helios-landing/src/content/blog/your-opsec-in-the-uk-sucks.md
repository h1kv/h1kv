> "OPSEC" - Operations Security is a process used to protect personal/sensitive information or by ensuring public information cannot be obtained through open-sourced intel (osint)
to all my **United Kingdom** friends, your opsec by default is practically __cooked__, just by knowing your name i shouldn't be able to know **your entire family tree and what your mother posted on facebook**
### But I do, here is how to mitigate.
Three data points is all it takes. Simple things as your name, age, general location, sibling-names is enough to trace you down to your birth certificate. 

Your records are public. **Legally** public and required by UK law to publish them. Knowing your birth name, year of birth, and other data points (such as your mother's maiden name) is just enough for me to order a valid birth certificate. 

### So how does one prevent this from occuring?
You can't. Your OPSEC is impossible once somebody online knows your real name. Although these public records sound innocent enough, there will be bad-actors who will be out there against you. Cross-checking, determination, and tons of information is enough for one to find things you think couldn't be found. 

From knowing someones name and DOB, I was able to figure out 600+ lines of information to his parents, siblings, and extended family. 

Alongside that, Facebook and all social platforms are practically the same too. People post, type messages, send photos, make things public with little to no thought regarding it. Sadly..
