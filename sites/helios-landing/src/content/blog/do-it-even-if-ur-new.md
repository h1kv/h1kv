> I have quite literally been successful in things that were entirely new to me and that would (in theory) take someone years of professional expereience to do. 
*edit: grammar fix (thanks to nikofrog)* 

# Why? 
I dont really know how you expect me to explain it; but here is a pros and cons list

---
**PROs**
- Learn something new regardless if you fuck it up or not
- Can come back to it again with a track record of doing it before 
- If it works, woohoo. If it don't then you still leave with something.
- Applying to a programme or something? This is proof that you decided to do something new regardless if the odds were against you 

**CONs**
- People might think your crazy

If you want to suceed in whatever it is the best thing for you to do is (excuse the quote) "to do it" and whether it falls into your category of what you like you still leave with experience, a track record of actually trying, and a chance to build those skills that you'll takeaway with once you do try to build something in your genuine interests. 

# For example 
15 year old me decided to start a DMA (digital marketing agency) with not an ounce of experience with marketing as a concept. If you were to ask me what Hubspot is or how to track KPIs I'd look at you and say no clue. 

However it worked. Secured some clients, made a genuine track record of doing things and creating impact. Even though its over now, I left with experience that I still use to this day.

# Also who doesn't like learning 
People learn different ways and I am more so of a visual learner and also a practical one. I will learn best by trying and failing at something. Sure reading concepts on how work with for instance the greedy algorithm in informatics/computer science is a way to learn - however actually applying what you learnt or just rawdogging it on a leetcode/codeforce problem refines it entirely. Just think of that on a much larger scale where you actually go serious on something even if you are completely a noob at it (hence the example of the DMA business I said above)

# Final words
I regret wasting my time and not building something - whether you build something with a team that doesn't even interest you, you still develop skills that can alter how you perform when you really need it. Thanks for reading:)
