# Things I like 

- **Computer Science.** Anything that is related to computers fascinate me to some degree. I also think hardware and embedded systems are cool. 
- **Startups.** I've worked at startups (*still do*). The energy and acceleration is my favourite part. It's quite rewarding too.
- **Reverse Engineering.** My first lines of code was doing this stuff on roblox games.
- **Biology & Psychology.** I regret not choosing these subjects in school. The human mind is still somewhat a black box like artificial intelligence. I don't think there's much difference in the difficulty of fully understanding how either arrives at its decisions.
- **3D Modelling.** Blender and CAD. I much prefer this over drawing on paper.
- **Science.** I like to read research papers. Like alot.
- **Photography.** I think every singular tech person I've met has a camera or just likes taking photos of things. Why? I don't know - If I find out why I'll edit this page and tell you. I love taking photos of nature and cool architecture. 
- **Video Games.** Don't play as often now but they were still a core part of my childhood. Shout out to [Rust](https://rust.facepunch.com) though.
- **AI.** Where do I even start?
- **Logistics.** The idea of optimizing how things move efficiently from A to B is something I love to nerd out about.
- **SCP.** When people on the internet decided to agree on a shared interest and make cool stuff about it. My favourite SCP being [SCP-018](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/scp-018); I also think the games made around them are absolutely amazing. [Alpha-1](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/task-forces#alpha-1) are also the goats.
- **Data Science.** The collection of data through OSINT (*open-source intelligence*) to determine results feels unreal. Heavily inspired by [Person Of Interest](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1839578/)
- **Space.** I do hope in my lifetime that science will let us explore more.
